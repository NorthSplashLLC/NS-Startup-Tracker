M HOLDINGS · SMART GROWTH OS — SHARED HUB BUILD

Key changes in this build:
- Detailing, commercial cleaning, pressure washing and landscaping/property maintenance now share larger regional service hubs.
- Added Landscaping & Property Maintenance as a scalable service company beginning in 2031.
- Shared hubs combine office/warehouse space, parking, storage, dispatch, admin and recruiting instead of duplicating those costs by brand.
- Hub count is capacity-aware: it follows the widest active city footprint and combined service-company headcount.
- Shared admin and recruiter staffing scales from combined service headcount, not one admin/recruiter per company office.
- Hub rent automatically moves from small to medium to large based on average employees supported per hub.
- New hub setup costs are paid only when another physical shared hub is actually required.
- North Splash market counts remain visible, but they no longer represent a separate paid office for every market.
- North Splash hire-date raises, D2D commission progression, vans, iPads, hotspots and payroll logic remain intact.
- Every scalable company continues profit-funded city/state expansion through 2047.
- Capital-heavy businesses still separate total project value from M Holdings cash equity.

This is a scenario-planning model, not a guaranteed forecast.
