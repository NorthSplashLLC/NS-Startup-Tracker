M HOLDINGS · SMART GROWTH OS

Key changes in this build:
- North Splash continues scaling through 2047 at a 20% annual detailer growth default after 2030.
- North Splash office density defaults to roughly one office per 16 detailers, producing 250+ offices by 2047 in the base scenario.
- Every other scalable business uses profit-funded expansion rather than a fixed "one location every N years" rule.
- Each venture keeps an expansion reserve funded by a configurable percentage of operating profit.
- New locations open only when the venture has enough accumulated expansion equity and remains under its annual growth cap.
- Capital-heavy businesses intentionally expand slower than service businesses.
- Added Markets tab with location, city, state and expansion-path visibility.
- Added editable controls for same-location growth, reinvestment %, annual location-growth cap, employees/location, max locations and expansion equity.
- Large real-estate/hospitality projects still separate total project value from M Holdings cash equity.
- Existing North Splash hire-date raise logic remains in place.

This is a scenario-planning model, not a guaranteed forecast.
