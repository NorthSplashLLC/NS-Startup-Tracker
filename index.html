Model rules

One van + two detailers + one D2D is the core revenue unit. The sales target is 4.3–5 completed details per day at a $220 average ticket, with 4.65/day used as the editable planning midpoint. D2D commission starts at 10% and rises by 1 percentage point every 3 months of that rep’s own tenure, capped at 15%, when the rep meets the performance threshold. Detailers and managers receive +$1/hour every 6 months from their own hire dates. April launches lean with no office, admin, recruiter, or dedicated manager; the owner manages until 5 vans. Starting in May, each office gets one $24/hour admin and recruiting support. Long-lived expansion purchases are separated from operating profit so the dashboard shows both profitability and actual cash needed for growth.
