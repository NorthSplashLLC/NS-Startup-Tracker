Model rules

One van + two detailers + one D2D is the core revenue unit. D2D commission starts at 10% and rises by 2 percentage points every 2 months of that rep’s own tenure, capped at 22%. Detailers and managers receive +$1/hour every 6 months from their own hire dates. One $24/hour admin is assigned per office. Recruiters begin in May 2027. Long-lived expansion purchases are separated from operating profit so the dashboard shows both profitability and actual cash needed for growth.
