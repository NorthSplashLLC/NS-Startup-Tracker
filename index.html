MODEL LOGIC

How the plan works

Founder phase: you knock doors on 3 off-days per week while your partner details on 3 off-days per week at $18/hour. Sales above fulfillment capacity become April backlog.

Scale phase: one van + two detailers + one D2D is the core revenue unit. D2D commission starts at 10% and rises 1 percentage point every 3 months when performance meets the threshold, capped at 15%. Detailers and managers receive +$1/hour every 6 months from their own hire dates. The owner manages the first crews; dedicated managers begin at 5 vans. Starting in May, offices add one $24/hour admin each plus recruiting support.
